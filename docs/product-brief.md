# Product Brief

## Name

Gwittim

The name comes from the Korean word "귀띔", meaning a quiet hint or discreet cue. The product should feel like someone gently helping from beside the user.

## Target Users

- Korean professionals in English meetings.
- Students in English lectures or seminars.
- Job seekers preparing for English interviews.
- Travelers or residents who need help in live English conversations.
- Customer-facing workers who need fast understanding and response support.

## Problem

Many non-native speakers can understand some English but lose the flow during fast conversations. The hardest moments are often real-time:

- The other person speaks too quickly.
- A question comes unexpectedly.
- The user understands the topic but cannot respond fast enough.
- Important details are forgotten after the call.

## Promise

Gwittim quietly listens, translates, summarizes, and suggests what to say next.

## MVP Scope

In scope:

- English speech to Korean subtitles.
- Original English transcript.
- Live rolling summary.
- Basic response drafting from Korean to natural English.
- Post-session summary and action items.

Out of scope for the first MVP:

- Direct phone-call audio capture.
- Voice cloning.
- Automatic participation as a meeting bot.
- Full offline translation.
- Legal/compliance automation.

## Success Metrics

- First partial transcript appears within 1 second after speech is detected.
- Korean translation appears within 2 seconds for short utterances.
- Users can follow a 10-minute English conversation without losing the main points.
- Users can produce at least one useful English response during a live session.
