# Privacy and Consent

Gwittim handles live conversation data. Privacy and consent are part of the product, not afterthoughts.

## Default Privacy Position

- Raw audio should not be stored by default.
- Audio should be processed transiently for transcription.
- Transcripts, translations, and summaries should be stored only with user consent.
- Users should be able to delete saved sessions.

## Consent UX

Before starting a session, Gwittim should clearly explain:

- Audio will be processed to generate transcript and translation.
- Other participants' voices may be included.
- The user is responsible for following local laws and getting required consent.
- The user can choose whether to save or discard the session.

## Legal Notes

Conversation recording, transcription, and monitoring laws vary by country and state. Some jurisdictions require consent from all participants.

Gwittim should not present itself as legal advice. The app should provide clear notices and controls that help users make lawful choices.

## Product Requirements

- Start session confirmation.
- Save history toggle.
- Delete session action.
- Privacy policy before public launch.
- Terms of service before public launch.
- Clear data retention settings.
